## Failure modes

Backtests usually fail from implementation details, not theory:

- **Lookahead bias**: using information unavailable at trade time.
- **Survivorship bias**: excluding delisted symbols.
- **Selection overfitting**: searching many ideas, reporting one winner.
- **Friction underestimation**: optimistic fills, zero slippage, no borrow constraints.
- **Regime dependence**: edge tied to one market environment.

## Better process

- Time-based train/validate/test splits.
- Walk-forward evaluation.
- Realistic transaction-cost and execution model.
- Deflated performance statistics and multiple-testing controls.
- Out-of-sample and paper-trading gates before production capital.

A useful rule: if the strategy only works with perfect fills and zero latency, it probably does not work.
